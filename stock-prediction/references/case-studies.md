# Case-Study Library

Eight cases: four winners/reversals (Micron, NVIDIA, SK Hynix-vs-Samsung, GOOGL), three failures (Super Micro, Intel 2024, plus Cisco/Moderna sketches), and one IPO-event study (SpaceX). Use them in Phase 3 as *analogs*: match on signal structure, not sector. Every case includes what was knowable ex-ante and with how much lead time.

**Bias disclosure (repeat it in reports):** these cases were selected because their outcomes were dramatic. Rules extracted from them overstate hit rates. Prices are approximate (cross-source variance ~1-3%).

## 1. Micron (MU) 2024-26 — the anchor case

**Move:** Aug 1, 2025 low $103.38 → Jun 25, 2026 ATH $1,213 (~10.7x in 10.5 months; +654% TTM as of Jul 2026). Crossed $1T market cap May 26, 2026.

**The predictive cluster, ranked by lead time:**

| Signal (date) | Lead to ignition (Sep 2025) / ATH (Jun 2026) | Taxonomy row |
|---|---|---|
| "HBM **sold out for calendar 2024 AND 2025, pricing already determined**" (Q4 FY24 call, Sep 25, 2024; stock ~$103) | ~12mo / ~21mo | Multi-quarter sold-out |
| Samsung HBM3E fails Nvidia qualification (Apr-May 2024, multiple outlets) | 16mo / 25mo | Competitor qual failure |
| Escalating reconfirmations: Mar 20, 2025 — sold-out again + HBM >$1B/qtr + TAM raised to $35B | ~6mo / ~15mo | Escalating serial reconfirmation |
| Q4 FY25 print (Sep 23, 2025): HBM ~$2B/qtr; AI-memory segment 5x YoY — growth *accelerating*, not continuing | inflection itself / ~9mo | The cyclical-recovery→supercycle discriminator |
| DRAM contract prices +30%, spot DDR5 ~4x in 3 months (Oct-Dec 2025, industry trackers) | leads Dec guidance by weeks | Third-party transaction data |
| Hyperscaler 2026 capex guides, combined +77% YoY (Jan-Feb 2026) | 3-4mo to parabolic leg | Buyer-side capex |
| HBM4 first-volume for Nvidia Vera Rubin (Q1 2026) — the 2024 playbook repeating into the next generation | 2-4mo | Own qualification win |

**Crucial discomfort:** after the single best signal (Sep 2024), the stock FELL ~20% over 3 months (Dec 2024 guidance miss on legacy NAND) and didn't ignite for a year. High-lead-time signals demand patience and get marked-to-market against you first.

**False signals that reversed (all were entry windows):** Dec 2024 legacy-segment guide miss (-16%; same report: data-center >50% of revenue first time); DeepSeek Jan 2025 (-11%); Liberation Day tariffs Apr 2025 ($76.77 — the best entry of the whole dataset); capex-raise selloff + Google TurboQuant compression-paper scare Mar 2026 (-20-30%, tripled in 2 months after); "Korean ETF warning" Jun 2026 (-13%, reversed in 48h).

**Lagging markers near the top:** UBS PT triple $535→$1,625 (May 26, 2026, ~85% of move done); consumer RAM-shortage press (Dec 2025); $1T market-cap milestone; Jul 7, 2026 "blowout that wasn't enough" (Samsung 19x profits → sector selloff) — unresolved at research time, flagged as possible regime flip.

## 2. NVIDIA (NVDA) 2022-25 — front-running a guidance blowout

**Move:** Oct 2022 low $11.21 (split-adj) → May 25, 2023 +24% day / $1T cap → Feb 2024 +$277B day → 2025: $4T (Jul), $5T (Oct), ATH $212.

**The observable ex-ante chain to the May 24, 2023 blowout (+53% guide vs consensus):**
1. ChatGPT launch (Nov 30, 2022) — NVDA +8.2% same day; market noticed but didn't reprice. Lead ~6mo.
2. Microsoft $10B OpenAI commitment (Jan 23, 2023) — first hard hyperscaler capital commitment. Lead ~4mo.
3. **NVDA rises on a -21% YoY revenue print** (Feb 22, 2023) on Huang's "AI at an inflection point" — the dissonance tell. Lead ~3mo.
4. GTC (Mar 21, 2023): H100 "full production," Meta/OpenAI/Stability named — public order-book evidence. Lead ~2mo.
5. GPU-shortage trade press escalates (Feb-Jul 2023). Continuous.
6. **Microsoft Q3 FY23 (Apr 25, 2023): Azure "demand remained stronger than available capacity"** — the highest-confidence tell, 29 days before the print.

Most of the tradeable edge concentrated in the final month; the earlier signals bought conviction, not timing.

**False signals (all recovered):** DeepSeek shock (Jan 27, 2025, -17%/-$589B — thesis inverted via Jevons paradox; recovered in ~5 months, then $4T→$5T); Sep 3, 2024 -$279B (capex-skepticism + DOJ subpoena — zero new demand data); four export-control episodes (each -5-8% day, trend resumed 1-3 quarters); Meta's capex cut (Feb 2023) as a bearish head-fake the same week as Microsoft's bullish language.

**Lagging:** TSMC "CoWoS is the shortage" commentary (Sep 2023) — 4 months after the repricing.

## 3. SK Hynix vs Samsung 2023-26 — positioning beats industry

**The gap:** TTM to Jul 2026: SK Hynix ~675-900% vs Samsung ~345-353% (~2x), riding the *identical* macro theme. FY2024: SK Hynix positive vs Samsung **-32%** — in the biggest AI-capex year ever. Jun 22, 2026: SK Hynix takes Korea's market-cap crown (first time since 2000). HBM share 2025: SK Hynix ~53-62%, Samsung ~17-21%, Micron ~19-21%.

**Ex-ante predictors of the gap:**
1. Samsung HBM3E qualification failure (Apr-May 2024) — reported while Samsung stock was still near its 2024 peak (Jul 11); preceded the Nov 2024 4-year low by ~6 months and the Oct 2024 public apology by ~5 months.
2. SK Hynix's *repeated* first-mover pattern: first HBM3 (Jun 2022, sole H100 supplier), first HBM3E (Mar 2024), first 12-layer (Sep 2024). Pattern-level, not one-off.
3. Samsung public apology (Oct 7-8, 2024) — capitulation converting suspicion into admission.
4. Q1 2025: SK Hynix overtakes Samsung in *overall DRAM revenue* for the first time since 1992 — the sub-segment story becomes the whole-company story.
5. Samsung finally passes qualification (Sep 2025): stock +5% day / +20% month — a bounce, not a re-rating. Confirmed by HBM4 allocations (Jun 2026): SK Hynix 60-70%, Samsung 25-30%.

**Lessons validated:** qualification news at the dominant customer = strongest transition signal; #1 in the hot sub-segment re-rates ~2x the industry; laggard catch-up = tradable bounce only (track allocation share for re-rating evidence); conglomerate dilution is real (Samsung Q1 2026: profit +756% YoY, stock fell same day on mobile noise); **late-cycle regime flip** — by Jul 2026 both stocks fell on good news; positioning rules dominate early/mid-cycle only.

**Laggard-recovery nuance:** Samsung's Oct 2024 apology came with a *named engineering fix* (HBM3E DRAM-core redesign) — that detail enabled the Sep 2025 pass and the 2026 catch-up rally (+158% YTD). Capitulation + named fix = 9-18 month catch-up trade; denial = no signal.

## 4. GOOGL 2023-26 — narrative-overhang reversal

**Move:** ~$89 (Jan 2023) → ~$360 (Jul 2026), ~+300%; +64.8% in 2025 alone; $4T cap Jan 2026. Inflection clustered Sep-Nov 2025.

**Four bear narratives and their fates:**
- **"ChatGPT kills search"** (peaked Feb 8, 2023, Bard flop, -$100B day): contradicted by *zero down quarters* of Search revenue 2023-2026. Died by attrition across ~10 consecutive beats — no single pop. Lead time for the divergence signal: ~24-30 months.
- **"Gemini behind OpenAI"**: resolved objectively by Gemini 2.5 Pro #1 on LMArena (Mar 25, 2025) — **8 months before** the market credited it (Gemini 3, Nov 18, 2025 → new ATH). Public leaderboards led sentiment.
- **DOJ antitrust / Chrome divestiture**: liability Aug 5, 2024 (confounded with the same-day global carry-trade crash); remedies **Sep 2, 2025 — no breakup → +9%, +$230B in a session**, the cleanest binary catalyst in the library. Legal analysts reading Mehta's liability opinion had flagged his proportionality reluctance ~13 months early. Note the asymmetry: worst-case was priced; a merely-bad outcome triggered a relief rally.
- **AI capex crushing FCF**: the *recurring* narrative — knocked the stock repeatedly (Feb 2025, Feb 2026, -10% Jun 22, 2026) and never permanently resolves. Fade the repeated panic only while backlog/demand accelerates (Cloud backlog $240B → $462B).

**Lagging confirmations:** Berkshire 13F (Nov 14, 2025, +5% — stock already +85% off April low); $4T milestone.

**Lessons:** binary legal events are calendarizable with asymmetric payoff; narrative-vs-numbers divergence is a slow but reliable signal; distinguish one-shot binary overhangs (buy resolution) from recurring ones (fade panics conditionally).

## 5. Super Micro (SMCI) 2024 — hype top with visible warnings

**Move:** parabolic to ~$1,229 intraday (Mar 2024, split-adj ~$122.90); then Hindenburg (Aug 27, 2024) → 10-K delay (Aug 28) → **auditor EY resigns** (Oct 30, -33% day) → by 2026, ~$26-28 (down ~98% from peak-equivalent) after federal smuggling charges (Feb-Mar 2026).

**Visible BEFORE the crash:**
1. 2020 SEC accounting-fraud settlement ($17.5M) — public record, repeat-offender base rate.
2. **Aug 6, 2024 earnings: gross margin 11.2% vs 17.0% year-ago — 430bp compression despite revenue ~3x YoY — disclosed 21 days before Hindenburg.** Growth bought with margin = quality-of-earnings flag.
3. Fwd P/E 52x vs 25x 3-yr average (Mar 2024) with margins *compressing*, not expanding.
4. Retail frenzy + S&P-add euphoria at the top.

**Lesson:** the bull case (real AI-server demand!) was true — and the stock still lost ~98%. Demand-thesis truth does not immunize against integrity/valuation failure. This is why the hype-top checklist runs even when the bull case is obvious. Also note the sequence: short-seller report (variable truth value) → *corroboration* by auditor resignation 2 months later (near-certain, but too late — most value already destroyed). Confirmation-grade signals are useless for prediction.

## 6. Intel (INTC) 2024 — the slow-motion warning chain

**Move:** Aug 1, 2024: -26% in a day (net loss, dividend suspension, 15%+ workforce cut); -52% YTD by December; CEO ousted Dec 2, 2024 (stock +4% on the ouster).

**The two-quarter public warning chain:**
1. **Jan 25, 2024**: Q4'23 *beat* — but Q1'24 guide $12.2-13.2B vs $14.2B consensus, EPS guide $0.13 vs $0.33; Data Center "double-digit sequential decline." Stock -10%. *Guidance overrides beats.*
2. **Apr 2-3, 2024**: first standalone foundry disclosure — **$7B operating loss in 2023**, breakeven "not before 2027." Stock -8%. Four months before the collapse.
3. Continuous: server share loss to AMD/NVDA in every quarterly dataset.

**Lesson:** collapses are often *serially telegraphed* — guide-down → segment-loss disclosure → crisis. Two consecutive quarters of deteriorating leading indicators is a bearish cluster; don't wait for the crisis print. Contrast with SMCI (abrupt, event-driven): both shapes exist. Epilogue: "Max Pessimism" (2024) → "Cautious Optimism" (late 2025, +80% off lows) — even failure cases mean-revert once expectations reset; a bearish call needs a horizon too.

## 7. Cisco 2000 & Moderna 2021-23 — sketches

**Cisco:** Mar 2000 peak P/E >200, EV/Sales 31; fell **-88% while revenue GREW** through 2002; ~20-25 years to reclaim the high. Multiple compression alone can produce catastrophic outcomes with zero business failure. Actively cited 2025-26 as the AI-infrastructure analog (NVDA at P/E ~45 vs Cisco's 200 — the gap between the analog and reality is itself informative both ways).

**Moderna:** ~$484-497 ATH (Aug 2021) → COVID revenue -94% YoY in Q2 2023 — *no fraud, no competitive loss* — demand simply normalized, and management's own guidance telegraphed it ahead of the market. The third failure shape: demand fade. Distinguish air-pocket from structural fade using independent demand data, not just company guidance.

## 8. SpaceX (SPCX) Aug 2026 — lock-up overhang relief, not automatic supply shock

**Set-up:** A newly public, thin-float company faced a known first lock-up release two trading days after its first Q2 report. The narrative said a large block becoming *eligible* to trade would create a forced supply shock.

**What was knowable:**

1. **Aug. 4, 2026 — earnings (COINCIDENT):** Q2 revenue was $7.8B, up 92% YoY; the net loss improved to $541M from $1.0B a year earlier. This was an operationally constructive print, although heavy AI/infrastructure spending remained the valuation and cash-flow risk.
2. **Aug. 5 — expectation positioning (COINCIDENT):** the stock fell nearly 14% as the market discounted the coming unlock, not a disclosed surge of sales.
3. **Aug. 6 — first unlock (COINCIDENT):** more than 900M shares became eligible to trade, roughly doubling the tradeable supply. The stock instead rose 6.1% to $114.92. The critical distinction: eligible to sell did not mean required to sell or evidence that insiders had sold.

**Correct reading:** This was a short-horizon **overhang-removal / expectations reset**. The Q2 improvement supplied buyers with a fundamental reason to absorb shares, while the feared mechanical event passed without the immediate flood the market had pre-priced. A thin float can amplify the reaction; do not assert a short squeeze without verified short-interest and covering data.

**What it did *not* prove:** It did not validate the long-term valuation, neutralize future lock-up tranches, or make CapEx irrelevant. The earnings print and the lock-up reaction are coincident signals, not the high-reliability multi-quarter demand evidence seen in the MU or NVDA cases.

**Reusable checklist for an IPO unlock:**

1. Read the prospectus: tranche date, price conditions, eligible shares, and whether an actual registered resale is involved.
2. Separate **anticipated supply**, **eligibility**, **confirmed sale filings**, and **real trading volume**.
3. Compare the event-day reaction with the pre-event move; a reversal after an anticipated unlock is meaningful for short-term positioning but not a fundamental re-rating by itself.
4. Treat low-float technicals cautiously. Do not fabricate mature moving averages or MACD history for a stock with only a few dozen sessions.
5. Keep future unlocks, the next earnings result, guidance, and CapEx/FCF as explicit invalidation triggers.
